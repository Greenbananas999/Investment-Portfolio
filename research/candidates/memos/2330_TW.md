**Thesis.** The world's leading contract chipmaker, making nearly all leading-edge logic chips (Apple, Nvidia, AMD, Broadcom); the AI build-out runs through its 3nm/2nm nodes and advanced packaging (CoWoS).

**Return drivers.** AI accelerator demand, the technology lead over Samsung and Intel Foundry, pricing power at the leading node, capex discipline, rising dividends.

**Risks.** Taiwan Strait geopolitics (the dominant tail risk, absent from every backtest); customer concentration (Apple, Nvidia); US tariffs and export controls; the semiconductor cycle (2022: -45% in USD).

**Verdict.** Strongest candidate. OOS Sharpe 0.96 and IR 1.13 with t = 3.4 and deflated Sharpe 0.78 make it the only one of the eight whose OOS edge largely survives the multiple-testing haircut; forward about 11% a year in USD, and the geopolitical tail argues for sizing, not exclusion.

> **How to explain this pick.** Since 2018 TSMC returned 32.2% a year in dollars with a worst fall of -46%, against 5.8% a year for the international-growth benchmark (risk-adjusted: Sharpe 0.96 vs 0.27). The TWD cost 1.0% a year against the dollar, so the local-currency return was 33.5%; hedging the currency each month would have raised the dollar return by 2.6% a year. Looking forward, the middle of our model estimates is about 11% a year in dollars over 7 years, with a wide 5-95% range of -5% to 29% a year.
